Sabbatical

July 2024 - Present

After over a decade of full time employment I decided to take some time for relaxation and personal development.

It has been great to spend a lot of time outside. I bikepacked the 650 mile Tree to Sea route on Vancouver Island with a friend in a blisteringly fast 8 days. I took several backpacking trips and many day hikes in the Cascade mountains near my home, including a 30 mile day to scramble Summit Chief mountain. Did a bunch of backcountry skiing, including a trip to Japan with friends. Became expert in foraging for delectable mushrooms: morels, chanterelles, and boletes.

Having my intellectual side free from "real work" I delved into a number of intellectual areas. I worked through part 1 of Programming Language Foundations in Agda and learned about functional programming and using proof assistants. I read Schrödinger's "What is Life?" which provided a very interesting perspective into the state of biological sciences shortly before the structure of DNA was discovered. I read a bit on quantum mechanics as well as molecular biology as well.

It’s been great to relax, and now I'm reinvigorated and excited to find my next job.
